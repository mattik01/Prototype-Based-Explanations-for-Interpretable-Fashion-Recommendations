## How the numbers may and may NOT be read

1. **No capacity control was run.** The identity block adds M·d = 200K params
   (+34% on this host). C − A therefore conflates mechanism with capacity.
   `feature_user_proto_noid_itemid_frozen` is registered and unqueued.
2. **No dot twin was run.** So a result cannot be attributed to the identity
   channel as opposed to the prototype head — which matters, because the head
   already costs fU ~0.068 on ml-1m (0.612 dot vs 0.544 proto, same composition).
   `lightfm_hist_itemid` is registered and unqueued.
3. **No measured noise band exists** for this combo. The literature-inherited
   +0.02 gate was dropped as unfounded (A5). A small positive is not a result
   until a ≥3-seed dev-tier band exists.
4. **Disconfirmation clause (A11) is binding:** no gain beyond the noise band on
   ml-1m = the accuracy bet failed. The "useful measurement instrument" framing
   in §5.2 may NOT be used to rescue it.
5. **Arm D's read-out split is not identified** (4b C9 / F-DC08-02) — with both a
   user-ID row and identity rows, an exact d-dimensional family swaps mass
   between them. Arm D is an accuracy number only.
6. What the C/D pair *does* answer on its own: **is a user-ID row still worth
   having once item-identity rows exist** (the D2 redundancy question). If
   D − C ≈ 0, the two unnamed channels are substitutes.
