#### Literature
- **From Clicks to Carbon: The Environmental Toll of Recommender Systems** (Vente et al., 2024)  
  - Measures CO₂ footprint of RecSys algorithms (2013 vs 2023)  
  - Finds deep learning RS emit ~42× more CO₂ than traditional methods  
- **Optimal Dataset Size for Recommender Systems** (Arabzadeh, 2025)  
  - Studies dataset downsampling vs accuracy & energy use  
  - Shows big energy/time savings with minimal accuracy loss  
- **Green Recommender Systems: Optimizing Dataset Size for Energy-Efficient Algorithm Performance** (Arabzadeh et al., 2024)  
  - Similar study, multi-dataset comparison  
  - Identifies which algorithms degrade gracefully with smaller data  
- **EMERS: Energy Meter for Recommender Systems** (Wegmeth et al., 2024)  
  - Library/tool for reproducible energy measurements in RS experiments  
- **e-Fold Cross-Validation for Recommender-System Evaluation** (Baumgart et al., 2024)  
  - Alternative to k-fold CV that reduces computational cost  
  - Maintains reliable evaluation while saving energy
